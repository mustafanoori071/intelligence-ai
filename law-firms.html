What is law firm intake automation?

Law firm intake automation connects your website, phone, and CRM so every new-matter inquiry gets the same screening flow — whether it arrives at 2 PM or 2 AM. Instead of a paralegal playing phone tag, automated legal intake asks practice-area questions, checks urgency, collects contact info, and books a consultation on your calendar. Your team gets a qualified lead in the pipeline, not a voicemail to chase tomorrow.

How AI legal intake works

  1. Visitor reaches out — website chat, contact form, or missed-call text-back (Growth plan).
  2. AI screens the matter — practice area, location, timeline, and your custom qualification questions.
  3. Lead syncs to CRM — every intake logged with tags, source, and screening answers.
  4. Consultation booked — qualified leads pick a time via Calendly; your team gets instant notification.

Ethics & compliance

Our automated legal intake collects information and books consultations. It does not provide legal advice, form an attorney-client relationship, or make case-value promises. We work with your firm to write intake scripts that match your bar's advertising rules, include required disclaimers where needed, and route urgent matters to on-call staff.
