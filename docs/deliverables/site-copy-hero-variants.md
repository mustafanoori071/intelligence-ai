# Homepage Hero Copy Variants — After-Hours Lead Loss

**Client:** Intelligence AI (getintelligence.co)  
**Audience:** HVAC and med spa owners missing leads after hours  
**Pain point:** Missed night calls, no after-hours coverage, leads going to faster competitors  
**Scope:** Hero headline, subhead, Book Now CTA only  
**Replaces:** Current hero — "Never Miss Another Lead Again" / generic local-service subhead  
**Date:** 2026-06-30

---

## Variant A — Blended (recommended for homepage)

**Vertical lean:** Blended (HVAC + med spa)

| Element | Copy |
|---------|------|
| **Headline** | Missed Calls After Hours Are Missed Money |
| **Subhead** | HVAC and med spa owners lose booked jobs every night because nobody picks up. We build your after-hours front office to capture leads, qualify them, and book appointments before they call your competitor. Done for you. Live in days. |
| **CTA** | Book Now |

**Rationale:** Opens with the site's own customer language ("missed calls = missed money"). Names both verticals in the subhead without splitting the headline. "Front office" matches glossary. "Done for you" and "live in days" pull from verified differentiators.
