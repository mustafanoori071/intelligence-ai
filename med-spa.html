What is medical spa lead generation?

Medical spa lead generation turns website visitors and social traffic into booked consultations. Instead of a contact form that sits in an inbox, med spa AI lead generation software answers FAQs instantly, asks about treatment interest, captures phone and email, and routes hot leads to your calendar — even when the front desk is closed.

How medspa lead management works

  1. Visitor lands on your site — from Google, Instagram bio link, or paid ads.
